## Rulings pre-registered at the M3 checkpoint (2026-09-06) — write into SPEC2.md

R7. **Cross-check threshold (amends pre-reg #7) is density-aware.** For a
    sampled hour with ≥ 3,000 ticks the threshold is 1.0 pip. For an hour
    with 500–2,999 ticks it is 1.0 pip + that hour's own median spread. Hours
    under 500 ticks are classified `UNVERIFIABLE` (the check cannot see
    them) — usable, tagged, and their usage before 2013 is a T5 decision on
    the by-year agreement evidence. Hours failing the applicable threshold
    remain `BLOCKED`. The roll window stays exempt.
R8. **Holiday eligibility.** The static major-holiday list in
    `config/calendar.toml` marks hours ineligible for execution in every
    backtest, in every year, regardless of whether the feed served data
    (pre-2013 the feed quoted through closed markets; those prices were not
    tradeable). The empties-derived calendar component is informational.
    This is a backtester rule to be implemented before T7; stated here.

## The battery (primary window 2015-01-01 → 2025-02-28, all 12 pairs, mids)

All statistics computed from stored bars (5m, 30m, 1h, 4h, 1d) built from
ticks; ticks used only for spread-by-session and density. Every figure
derived at render time (R6). Every test run is a ledgered trial; the report
states trial counts next to any claim; where p-values are reported across
pairs × horizons, apply and state a false-discovery correction.

1. **Return distributions by horizon**: moments, tail ratios, normality
   diagnostics; how tails change with horizon.
2. **Stationarity and memory**: unit-root sanity on prices/returns;
   variance-ratio profiles across horizons (the trend-vs-mean-reversion
   fingerprint); return autocorrelation by lag and horizon; sign
   persistence. Report effect sizes, not just significance.
3. **Volatility**: clustering (autocorrelation of |r| and r²); regime
   terciles by realised vol; vol by hour-of-day, day-of-week, session;
   how memory statistics differ across vol regimes.
4. **Session and spread structure**: returns, vol and spread by session
   (derived boundaries; R3 — spread comparisons control for ticks per
   hour); the roll window characterised as its own regime (pre-reg #4
   evidence); spread vs vol relationship.
5. **Stability — the load-bearing section**: every headline statistic
   above recomputed split-half (2015-01→2019-12 vs 2020-01→2025-02) and
   on rolling 2-year windows; report sign and rank stability. A property
   that flips is reported as unstable, not averaged. Appendix: the same
   memory and vol statistics on 1h/1d bars across the full history
   (2005+, AUDUSD 2011+), era-tagged per R7's agreement table, to show
   which properties survive the 2000s.
6. **Tick density (R4)**: the density series by pair × year × session;
   relationship to realised vol, spread, and the T1/T2 known feed changes;
   structural-break candidates. Conclude explicitly whether tick count is
   usable as an activity proxy and under what conditions.
7. **The 312 unexplained-empty dates (from T3)**: characterise by pair,
   weekday, year; classify as feed artefact / partial holiday / unknown.
