## The battery (mids; 1h, 4h, 1d bars; primary window, with the 2009-2012 era as a stability check)

Every test ledgered as a trial; families and BH correction stated; effect
sizes alongside significance; every headline statistic split-half and
rolling-2y stable/unstable per T4's discipline.

1. **Correlation structure**: pairwise return correlations by horizon;
   stability over rolling windows; regime dependence (vol terciles from
   prior vol, as T4); the correlation *network* — clusters, and how they
   change in high-vol regimes (do correlations "go to one"?).
2. **Currency-strength decomposition**: factor each pair's returns into
    currency components (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, AUD, CAD, NZD) via the
    triangular structure of the universe; variance explained per currency;
    idiosyncratic residual per pair; stability. Report whether currency
    factors carry memory the pairs themselves do not (autocorrelation and
    variance ratios on the factor series, same tests as T4, same
    correction).
3. **Cointegration scans**: Engle-Granger and Johansen on all pairs-of-pairs
    and economically motivated triples, at 1h/4h/1d, WITH: a stated
    multiple-testing correction across the scan; half-life of the spread;
    out-of-window confirmation (a relationship found in 2015-2019 must
    hold, untouched, in 2020-2025 and in 2009-2012 to be reported as
    stable); the spread's cost-to-move ratio at the reference notional
    (both legs' round trips vs the spread's typical move — T5's arithmetic
    applied to the pair-of-pairs). Report the trial count next to every
    surviving relationship.
4. **Lead-lag**: cross-correlation at lags 1-12 bars for every ordered pair
    at each horizon, BH-corrected; effect sizes in bp; stability; whether
    any lead survives the T5 cost floor of the *lagging* pair at the
    reference notional.
5. **Portfolio-level geometry**: given the correlation structure, the
    diversification available across the universe at each horizon (e.g.
    effective number of independent bets by regime) — the input a
    portfolio-level T7 evaluation needs.
6. **Characterisation only, 5m/30m**: correlation and lead-lag summaries,
    no hypotheses (D7).
